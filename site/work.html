Four things that happen at work

Built with Stageshow by Benji Taylor (MIT), in the distinctUI look. Each plays once when you scroll to it. With reduced motion you see the finished frame.

Sign, in order

The requester sits apart. The last ring is dashed until she signs, then the link goes solid and the count rolls.

Send many at once

Tick two rows and the black bar swaps in. One press changes both, and a notice with Undo stays.

Command menu

⌘K on a white veil. Typing narrows the list, Enter runs the top match, and the page says what changed.

A report

Four black lines on one scale draw in turn. The switch turns them into the table they came from.

Nine extra rules

The twelve rules still hold. These are what an app adds.

  1. 1
    Dense, then roomy if you like. Three row heights, Compact, Default and Airy, remembered per person. 34, 44 or 56 pixels.
  2. 2
    States are drawn. Hover is grey-50, selected and focused rows get a black edge. Status is a shape plus words: solid, half, dashed, or a black !
  3. 3
    One ring colour per person. Everywhere in the product. Photos, never initials, in marks under 28 pixels.
  4. 4
    Your colour stays on your mark. A customer's brand colour goes on their workspace mark only. Nothing else in the app changes colour.
  5. 5
    Who asked is not who signs. In an approval the requester sits apart from the chain, and dashed means still to sign.
  6. 6
    Personal details are need-to-know. IP addresses and places show to Admin and Auditor only (POPIA). Dates are full, with the time zone.
  7. 7
    Shortcuts you can see, and switch off. Single keys show as key caps and turn off with one switch (WCAG 2.1.4). ⌘K always works.
  8. 8
    Undo instead of are you sure. Bulk actions happen, then a notice with Undo stays in the page. Only what can't be undone asks you to type the name.
  9. 9
    Compare in black. Small multiples on one shared scale, never a colour per series. Every chart can become its table.

Use it

Load the work layer after distinctUI. It adds the app shell, dense tables, the command menu, approvals, charts and settings, and the script that makes them move.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="distinctui.css">
<link rel="stylesheet" href="distinctui-work.css">
<script src="distinctui.js" defer></script>
<script src="distinctui-work.js" defer></script>

Or ask Claude with the skill installed: “Build an approvals screen with distinctUI@work.” The rules are in work.md.
