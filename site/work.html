Four things that happen at work

Built with Stageshow by Benji Taylor (MIT), in the distinctUI look. Each plays once when you scroll to it. With reduced motion you see the finished frame.

Sign, in order

The requester sits apart. The last ring is dashed until she signs, then the link goes solid and the count rolls.

Send many at once

Tick two rows and the black bar swaps in. One press changes both, and a notice with Undo stays.

Command menu

⌘K on a white veil. Typing narrows the list, Enter runs the top match, and the page says what changed.

A report

Four black lines on one scale draw in turn. The switch turns them into the table they came from.

Nine extra rules, each one live

The twelve rules still hold. These are what an app adds, numbered W1 to W9, each with the pieces that keep it. Open “Show markup” under any of them to copy it.

W2

States are drawn.

Hover is a grey wash, and the open or selected row gets a black edge. Status is a shape plus words: dashed for a draft, half for in review, solid for done, a black ! when it's stuck. Loading shows grey shapes, after 300ms.

Seventeen templates

Every piece above, put together into the screens of four apps at one company.

Home

What needs you, what's moving, what's stuck.

Contracts table

Saved views, sort, select rows, a bulk bar, Undo.

Inbox

A list beside the open item, ⌘K, key caps.

Approval

Who asked apart from who signs, then the trail.

Reports

Black small multiples on one scale, a heat map.

Audit log

Need-to-know details, with View as.

Settings

Roles in place, single sign-on, keys shown once.

States

Loading, empty, broken, not allowed, too long.

Trust page

Where data lives, who can see it, POPIA.

Sign in

The company's own sign-in, then a workspace, each with its own colour.

Purchase request

The rules beside the form, who'll sign worked out, the one thing to fix.

Invoice check

Contract, order, delivery and invoice side by side; a bank change called out.

People and roles

Synced from the directory, roles changed in place, seats, invites.

Hiring board

Intake: a column per stage, a button on every card, no photos while screening.

Support desk

Kettle: the queue beside the case, a dashed team note, what you can't see.

Shift rota

Roster: people by days, cover counted with a black !, leave and what it changes.

Driver's phone

Roster on the road: one stop at a time, big buttons, offline said plainly.
