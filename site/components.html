Quotes and testimonials

A pull quote: light, one bold word, who said it under it in their ring. One per page section at most.

We keep your bookings for as long as your account is open, and delete them 30 days after you close it.

From the Tilde privacy page

A quote inside running text: a black edge and grey-800 words. Never a coloured bar or a giant quotation mark.

Testimonials: two or three cards of quotes. Customers wear the plain black ring, since the colour belongs to your mark. No star ratings in gold, no logos in colour.

Dates and amounts

Pick a date from today on.

The native date field, so phones get their own wheel. The calendar button opens a month grid under it. Days you can't pick are grey and crossed out, and the hint says why.

The month grid on its own, for bookings. Today has a grey edge, the day you pick is black. Arrow keys move by day and week, Page Up and Page Down by month.

A quantity stepper: minus and plus around a number you can also type. At the limit the button fades. In a cart, going to zero takes the item out.

Announcements and cookies

New: book a chair from your phone. See how

One line at the very top of the page, for news worth a click. It closes for good: give it data-dismiss-key and it stays closed.

Cookies. One keeps you signed in. With your OK, another counts visits so we can see which pages help. What we use

The cookie notice sits small in the bottom corner, never a wall over the page, with no veil behind. The black pill is the choice that shares least. Read the answer with distinctUI.dismissed("cookies").
