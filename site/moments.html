Moments

One thing happens

Small product stories for a hero, a feature card or a docs page. A believable screen in black, white and grey, and one change you can watch: a ring going live, a follower landing, an ask answered, a field fixed.

Built with Stageshow by Benji Taylor, in the distinctUI look. Each plays once when you scroll to it. With reduced motion you see the finished frame.

Going live

Press, the pill settles on itself, the dashes fill in with one pop, the version rolls, the update arrives and carries the list down, and a follower lands a beat later.

Make your own

Install both skills, then ask Claude for a moment. distinctUI brings the look and its rules; Stageshow brings the stage, the cursor, framing and the motion parts.

npx skills add benjitaylor/skills --skill stageshow

Then: Build a distinctUI moment for our pricing page: a plan changes and the total rolls.

  1. 1
    Light, mono, the distinctUI look. <Stage className="dui-look" palette="mono"> with stageshow-look.css. No dark, glass or blueprint themes.
  2. 2
    Colour only on rings. Put real .dui-mark elements in the stage. Status is said in words, not green or red.
  3. 3
    Once, not a loop. play="once" when seen, with Play again. The poster is the payoff.
  4. 4
    One thing happens. Three to five beats, three to seven real words, filler bars for the rest.
  5. 5
    Every change moves. Numbers roll, buttons confirm on themselves, rows open their room. Nothing swaps or pops.
