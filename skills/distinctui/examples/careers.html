Good questions

Do I need a degree?

No. Show us something you made, or tell us about a problem you fixed.

What happens to my CV?

Only the people hiring for that role see it. We delete it six months after the role closes, unless you ask us to keep it.

Can I apply in isiZulu or Afrikaans?

Yes. Write in the language you think in.
