Using Tilde

Your book, your clients and your prices are yours. We run the booking page and send the reminders.

Tilde is run by Tilde (Pty) Ltd in Johannesburg. You can use it if you're 18 or older and run a shop, a chair or a mobile service. One account is for one business; staff get their own logins on the Shop plan.

Don't use Tilde to take bookings for anything illegal, or to message people who didn't book with you. If you do, we'll tell you why and close the account.

Paying

One chair is free. Paid plans are monthly, and you can stop any time before the next month starts.

Prices are in rand and include VAT. We charge on the same day each month and email the invoice. If a payment fails, we try again for 7 days before moving you back to the free plan. Nothing gets deleted.

Deposits your clients pay go straight to your bank account through our payment partner, less their fee of 2.9%. We never hold your money.

Leaving

Download everything with one button, then close your account. We delete it all within 30 days.

Settings, Your data gives you your clients, bookings and invoices as a spreadsheet. Closing your account cancels your plan the same day. Clients with bookings get one SMS saying the booking page is closed.

You, your staff, and three companies that help us run Tilde: our host (servers in Cape Town), our SMS sender and our payment partner. Each one may use your data only to do that job for us. A court order is the only other way anyone gets it, and we'll tell you unless the order says we can't.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Most of this you can do yourself in Settings. For anything else, write to our information officer, Zanele Khoza, at privacy@tilde.example. She answers within 5 working days.

Not happy with the answer? You can complain to the Information Regulator.
