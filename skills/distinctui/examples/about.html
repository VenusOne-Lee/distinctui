About us

Small software for the shops on your street.

Nine people in Braamfontein, making tools that work on a cheap phone and a bad signal.

Why we started

In 2023 our founder's aunt ran out of bread on a Saturday morning for the third week in a row. She counted stock in a school exercise book, and the book was always a day behind.

Every stock app we found wanted a laptop, a card and fibre. So we built one that listens to you count out loud, works offline and costs less than a loaf a week. Then shop owners asked for three more things, and we built those too.

So far

2,340
shops counting with us, in seven provinces
4 apps
stock, lay-bys, supplier orders and a till
