What your security team asks

Something else? Our security lead answers in two working days.

Where is our data kept?

In Johannesburg, on AWS af-south-1, unless you choose Frankfurt on Enterprise. Backups stay in the same country as the data.

Who at Quarry can see our contracts?

Nobody, by default. If you ask for help, you can let one support person in for 24 hours. Every look is in your audit log.

How do you meet POPIA?

We're your operator under POPIA. IP addresses and places are only shown to your Admins and Auditors, and we delete them after the time you set.

Can we run our own security test?

Yes, once a year, with two weeks' notice. Ask your account manager for the rules of the test.

What happens to our data if we leave?

Export everything as PDF and CSV at any time. We delete it 30 days after your plan ends, and email you when it's done.
