# Voice

The words are part of the design. distinctUI pages sound like a capable friend who builds things: plain, warm, specific.

## Rules

1. **Write from the reader's side.** Name things by what people recognise ("your page", "people you follow"), never by how the system works ("profile entity", "subscription feed").
2. **Full, short sentences.** Active voice. One idea each.
3. **Buttons say exactly what happens.** "Share a launch", "Send for review", "Try it". Not "Submit", "OK" or "Click here".
4. **Headings carry one bold word** that holds the meaning: "Just **shipped**", "Builder of the **week**", "Help **wanted**".
5. **Errors name the field and the fix.** "Add a link that starts with https://" beats "Invalid URL". No apologies, no "Oops".
6. **Empty states say what will appear here and how to get the first one.** "Your launches show up here once they're live. Share your first one."
7. **Say what's optional.** Put "(optional)" in grey after the label, rather than an asterisk on what's required.
8. **No promises you can't keep.** Prefer "Visibility, not prizes" to "Get discovered by thousands".
9. **Sentence case everywhere.** Including buttons, tabs and headings.
10. **Avoid**: exclamation marks, emoji as icons, "simply", "just" as filler, "seamless", "unlock", "supercharge", em-dash asides, "not X but Y" framing.

## Examples

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| Submit | Send for review |
| Oops! Something went wrong. | That link didn't open. Check it starts with https:// and try again. |
| No data | Nothing here yet. People you follow show up here when they ship. |
| Email* | Email |
| Phone | Phone (optional) |
| Unlock premium features! | (Don't.) |
| Successfully saved! | Saved. |
