The builder for the distinctUI theme. Your content first, three designs that follow the rules, and your own Claude to build with.

== Description ==

distinctUI+ is for people without a designer. You type what you'd say about your business once. It lays the same words out three ways, Calm, Bold and Step by step, and every one follows the twelve distinctUI rules. Pick one and keep building on a full-screen canvas:

* The page in the middle, drawn with your site's own CSS, with your phone beside it.
* Click a section to try its other layouts. Type straight on the page. Double-click a word to make it the bold one.
* A rule line along the bottom says when something breaks a rule, in plain words, and fixes it for you if you want.
* Every publish is a version (v0.1, v0.2 and so on). Go back to any of them. Changes you haven't published stay a draft; the live page doesn't move until you publish.

Sections: hero, proof (people in their rings), features, steps, numbers, pricing, questions, call to action, form, products and posts.

= Forms =

The Form section is a contact form, booking request or waitlist. Entries land under distinctUI+, Entries, and you get an email. Spam is kept out with a hidden field and a limit of five sends in ten minutes. If you already use Contact Form 7, WPForms or Gravity Forms, the section can hold one of their forms instead.

= Build with your Claude =

distinctUI+ gives Claude abilities through the WordPress Abilities API and the WordPress MCP Adapter: read the guide, list and read pages, check a spec, show three designs, make, change and move sections, set the signature, publish and go back to a version. Claude writes a page spec, never HTML, so it can't break the look. Settings, Build with your Claude has the setup to paste into Claude. The AI runs on your own Claude account; distinctUI+ doesn't charge for it or see it.

You can also leave notes for Claude on the canvas ("Add three plans: Solo R149, Shop R399, Group R899"). Claude picks them up the next time you ask it to check.

= Works with =

* WooCommerce: the Products section shows your products, with their prices and Add to cart.
* Yoast SEO, Rank Math and SEOPress: the page's title and description go to them, without overwriting anything you typed there. With no SEO plugin, distinctUI+ prints the description and share tags itself.
* WP Rocket, LiteSpeed Cache, W3 Total Cache, WP Super Cache, WP Fastest Cache, SiteGround Optimizer and Breeze: publishing clears the page.
* WPML and Polylang: a translation starts as a copy of the page, then each language is built on its own.
* Contact Form 7, WPForms and Gravity Forms: see Forms.

The look of those plugins comes from the free distinctUI theme.

= If you stop paying =

Pages are written as ordinary WordPress blocks. They stay live and unchanged without a licence, and without the plugin. You can still edit them in the block editor. The canvas opens to show you your pages, but changing them needs a licence.

== Installation ==

1. Install and activate the distinctUI theme (Appearance, Themes).
2. Upload distinctui-plus.zip under Plugins, Add New, Upload Plugin, and activate it.
3. Add your licence key under distinctUI+, Settings.
4. distinctUI+, New page.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does it work with other themes? =

The canvas does, but pages are drawn with the distinctUI theme's styles, so they look right only with it active.

= Can I change colours and fonts? =

No. Colour lives only on your mark; that's the system. Pick a ring palette and your signature colour under Appearance, distinctUI.
